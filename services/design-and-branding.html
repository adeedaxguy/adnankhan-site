FAQs

The questions every founder asks first.

How is this different from Fiverr or a bargain logo? +
A bargain logo is one file. A brand identity is a system — typography rules, color usage, photography direction, voice — designed to make every future asset look like it belongs to the same brand. The bargain logo gets you started. The system is what you actually use for the next five years.
How long does a full rebrand take? +
A complete identity system typically takes 4–6 weeks. If we're rebuilding the site too, plan for 6–10 weeks end-to-end. Logo refresh only is usually 2 weeks.
What does it effort? +
Identity systems, web-design + identity bundles, and standalone refreshes are all scope-led, scoped to what you need. You get the exact plan after the strategy call — no open-ended billing.
Do you handle development too? +
Yes — and most clients bundle them. Designing a Shopify store and then handing it off to a separate developer usually loses 20–30% of the design intent. When I do both, you get a site that ships exactly as designed.
Will I own the source files? +
All of them. Figma source files, font licenses (paid for under your name), exported assets in every format you need, brand guidelines doc. Yours forever — no licensing terms, no vendor lock.
