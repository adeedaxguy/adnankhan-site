Sprint Readiness

When a landing page sprint is the right move.

A sprint is the right next step when an audit found message-match, proof, form, mobile-first-screen, or CTA friction and the offer is clear enough to ship. It is not the route for crawl, index, canonical, or schema blockers; those belong in a technical SEO audit.

Good sprint fit

  • One audience and one primary offer are already known.
  • Traffic source is defined: organic, paid, partner, email, or outbound.
  • The page needs a stronger first screen, proof sequence, and lead path.
  • Analytics should show audit runs, form intent, calls, WhatsApp clicks, and qualified enquiries.

Needs audit first

  • The offer changes every week or has several unrelated buyer types.
  • The site gets visits but no lead signals or no visible conversion path.
  • Search Console shows impressions for audit, compatibility, or service terms but the matching page is unclear.
  • The next step is not obvious to a non-technical buyer.

If the fit is unclear, start with the free website audit report. It will show whether the first move should be a sprint, a structure fix, or a deeper SEO/AEO compatibility cleanup.

FAQs

Sprint questions, answered.

What if I need more revisions? +
Each sprint includes 2 revision rounds (one on design, one on dev). More than that means we redesign the contract — usually a small add-on for another full revision cycle. The fixed scope is what keeps the scope predictable.
What platforms do you build on? +
Whatever you already use. Shopify (Liquid section), WordPress (custom template or Elementor), Webflow, Framer, or hand-coded HTML for static. If you're building from scratch I'll recommend the right platform for your case.
Do you write the copy? +
I write copy direction — the hero, structure, CTA framing, and section headlines. If you need a full copywriter for body content, I'll either work with one you already use or recommend trusted partners. Pure copywriting isn't in scope.
Can I extend the sprint? +
Yes. Common extensions: A/B test setup, Klaviyo email sequences, and additional pages on the same project — each scoped separately.
What conversion rate should I expect? +
Depends entirely on your offer, traffic source, and buyer match. I won't promise a number — but I'll structure the page so you can measure clearly and iterate. The page is the conversion engine; the strategy on top of it is what wins or loses.
