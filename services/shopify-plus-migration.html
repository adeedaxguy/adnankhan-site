FAQs

Migration questions, answered honestly.

How long does a Shopify Plus migration take? +
6–10 weeks depending on store complexity. Small stores (under 1,000 SKUs, standard apps) usually land at 6 weeks. Stores with custom B2B logic, subscriptions, or complex pricing tables run 8–10. The audit phase gives you the exact number before you commit.
What does a migration cost? +
$8K–$25K fixed-fee for most migrations. Pricing scales with SKU count, custom logic, and integrations — not with timeline. You get the exact number after the audit phase, in writing, before work starts.
Will my SEO survive? +
Yes — that's the central engineering problem and where most migrations fail. Every URL gets mapped 1:1 (or to its closest semantic equivalent), structured data is rebuilt, and we monitor CrUX + Search Console daily for 30 days post-launch. The contract specifies SEO performance must hold within 5% of baseline within 60 days.
What apps survive the migration? +
Klaviyo, Recharge, Yotpo, Judge.me, Postscript, Gorgias — all carry over cleanly. Anything Shopify-specific you currently use that's not on Plus needs a replacement plan, which we document in the audit. About 80% of your stack typically transfers without surgery.
Can I do this without zero downtime? +
Yes, but zero downtime is the default — the cost difference is small once the playbook is run regularly. We schedule the DNS swap for your lowest-traffic hour (usually 3–5am local) and keep the old store warm as fallback for 72 hours.
