Who Webflow is for

Built for the kind of site that earns its CMS.

Webflow isn’t the right answer for every project. When it’s the right answer, it’s the cheapest path to a marketing site your team will actually maintain.

Webflow is right for you if

  • +Marketing site for a SaaS, B2B, or service business
  • +Brand-led editorial site where animation and interaction matter
  • +A marketing or content team that needs to ship new pages without filing a developer ticket
  • +Light commerce (under 200 SKUs, no complex variant logic)
  • +A budget that says “do it once, do it right” instead of “cheapest first”

Webflow is the wrong call if

  • −You need real e-commerce with subscriptions, complex variants, or B2B pricing — use Shopify
  • −You need deep editorial workflows or 500+ posts with author roles — use WordPress
  • −You need a custom backend, login flows, or member-only content at scale
  • −You’re trying to save $40/month on hosting — the platform fee is the deal
  • −You have no one who will ever edit it — static HTML would be cheaper

FAQs

The honest answers founders ask first.

Why pick Webflow over WordPress or a custom build? +
Because the people who edit the site after launch aren’t developers. Webflow gives your team a visual editor that respects the design system, with native hosting and CDN included. For SaaS, B2B and brand-led marketing sites, that combination beats WordPress in maintenance and beats a custom build in handoff time. The trade-off: less custom backend logic, lower SKU ceiling for commerce.
What does a typical Webflow build cost? +
Marketing sites land between $3.5K and $9K, fixed-price. CMS-heavy sites with dynamic templates, collections and rich interactions land between $7K and $15K. WordPress→Webflow migrations price separately after a content audit. You’ll get the exact number after the 15-minute scope call, with milestones written into Upwork escrow before I start.
Do you handle design too, or only the Webflow build? +
Both. I can take a Figma file and ship a pixel-accurate Webflow build, or take a brief and design + build the whole thing end-to-end. Most engagements are the second kind — the design and the build talking to each other from day one usually yields a better result than either in isolation.
Can you migrate my existing WordPress / Squarespace site to Webflow? +
Yes — this is one of the more common engagements. URL structure preserved, 301 redirects mapped, SEO carried over, content migrated into a proper CMS schema. Typical migration: 3–4 weeks for a 30-page marketing site. The post-migration audit is included.
What about the Webflow hosting fee? +
Webflow’s site plans run $14–$39/month depending on traffic and CMS items. That’s the deal — you’re paying for hosting, CDN, SSL, version history, and the editor. For most marketing sites it’s cheaper than WordPress when you factor in the hosting, security plugins, and CDN you’d otherwise stack.
What about SEO on Webflow? +
Webflow gives you proper control over titles, descriptions, alt text, canonical tags, structured data, and sitemap generation. The output HTML is clean and crawlable. The one thing to watch: Webflow doesn’t natively support some structured-data types that WordPress plugins offer — for those I drop custom JSON-LD into the page head. That’s a 10-minute job per template.
