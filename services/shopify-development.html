FAQs

The questions every smart founder asks first.

What is custom Shopify theme development? +
Custom Shopify theme development means building or heavily tailoring the storefront code, templates, sections, blocks, Liquid, CSS, JavaScript, metafields, product pages, and performance foundation around the brand and buying journey instead of relying on a generic prebuilt theme.
When should a Shopify store use a custom theme? +
A custom theme makes sense when the store needs stronger brand differentiation, faster mobile pages, custom product logic, better merchandising, cleaner sections, fewer app workarounds, or a checkout and PDP experience designed around paid traffic and repeatable conversion.
Can you rebuild a Shopify theme without changing the brand? +
Yes. A rebuild can preserve the visual identity while replacing fragile templates, slow scripts, duplicate sections, weak product page structure, and hard-to-edit Liquid with a cleaner Online Store 2.0 architecture.
Do you work with US and UK clients remotely? +
Yes. The process is async-first, documented, milestone-led, and built for remote founders who need clear scope, fast decisions, and clean handoff.
What if I'm not technical? +
Most of my best clients aren't. You bring the brand and the customer obsession. I translate that into a store your team can actually run — with a 1-hour handoff session and written Loom walkthroughs of every section.
What happens after launch? +
30 days of post-launch support is included on every project. After that, most clients move to a lightweight ongoing support plan so I stay on call for new sections, A/B tests, and seasonal launches.
