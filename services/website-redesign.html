When redesign is the right move

Redesign around a business constraint, not a fashion cycle.

Publishing, integrations, mobile behaviour, performance, or platform constraints create recurring operational friction.

A rebrand, replatform, consolidation, or content rewrite may remove valuable routes unless decisions are mapped before launch.

FAQs

Website redesign questions, answered.

How do you protect SEO during a website redesign? +
We inventory valuable URLs, queries, conversions, links, and content before deciding what to keep, improve, merge, or redirect. Canonicals, metadata, schema, internal links, analytics, sitemaps, robots directives, and live responses are verified after launch.
What should we audit before redesigning? +
Pages and queries already earning visibility, buyer paths that create enquiries, forms, proof, analytics, URL dependencies, performance, accessibility, schema, content ownership, and the release path.
Can you keep our existing visual identity? +
Yes. The scope can preserve the approved identity while improving responsive behaviour, page hierarchy, content structure, technical foundations, and the conversion journey.
Will a redesign guarantee more leads or rankings? +
No. The work can remove measurable friction and protect important search signals, but outcomes also depend on demand, competition, authority, offer fit, implementation, traffic quality, and observation time.
