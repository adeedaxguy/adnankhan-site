Market my website: a practical plan for getting found and turning visits into enquiries.

Most websites do not need random posts. They need the right page architecture, search visibility checks, technical cleanup, stronger landing pages, and a conversion path that tells visitors what to do next.

Short answer: To market a website, start with Search Console evidence, fix index and technical issues, improve pages already getting impressions, build missing landing pages, then measure enquiries and calls.
