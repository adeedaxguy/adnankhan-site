Project notes

What had to work for Boston Concierge Orthopedics

Boston Concierge Orthopedics sits in a service journey where the site has to make the offer credible quickly. The page experience had to help visitors need to understand what is being offered, why it is reliable, and how to take the next step. That meant treating the build as more than a visual refresh: the structure, copy hierarchy, technical setup, and handoff all had to support the same commercial job.

Build priorities

  • Clarify the first screen so the visitor understands the offer before they scroll.
  • Support the page with proof, structure, and a path to enquire without unnecessary friction.
  • Keep the site maintainable so future edits do not require a full rebuild.
