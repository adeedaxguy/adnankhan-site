Access and indexation

  • Important pages load successfully and return the intended status.
  • Robots directives, canonicals, redirects, and sitemap URLs agree.
  • Navigation and contextual links lead to the preferred final URL.
  • Duplicate, alternate, redirected, and deliberately excluded pages are classified instead of treated as automatic errors.

Structure, content, and AI-search clarity

  • Each page has one clear job, title, H1, direct answer, and next action.
  • Important entities, services, audiences, constraints, and proof are stated in visible language.
  • Headings and internal links make the topic hierarchy understandable.
  • Structured data matches visible content and does not invent ratings, reviews, locations, or pricing.

Trust and conversion path

  • The first screen explains the offer and gives one obvious action on mobile.
  • Case studies and claims are verifiable and relevant to the service.
  • Phone, WhatsApp, form, booking, and checkout paths work from start through confirmation.
  • Confirmed submissions and meaningful clicks are measured without counting failed attempts as leads.

What this template cannot decide by itself

A template cannot verify whether a warning matters to your stack, which page deserves priority, or whether a redesign will improve lead quality. Use evidence from the live site and classify each finding as fix, expected/no action, or monitor.

FAQ

Can I download this website audit report template as a PDF?

Yes. Use your browser's print command and choose Save as PDF. The template is designed to remain useful as a working document or client-facing PDF.

What should every website audit finding include?

Include the affected URL, evidence, business impact, priority, owner, recommended action, acceptance criteria, and validation status.
