4. Voice

Editorial. Direct. Earned authority.

We sound like

  • A senior engineer explaining their work over coffee.
  • Specific numbers. Specific outcomes. Specific tradeoffs.
  • Long sentences when warranted. Short sentences when they hit.
  • Opinionated. Willing to say no. Willing to say this is a bad idea.
  • Quiet confidence — the proof is in the work, not the language.

We don’t sound like

  • An agency pitch deck. “We empower brands to scale through…” — no.
  • A junior dev imitating Twitter threads.
  • Aspirational marketing fluff. “World-class.” — never.
  • Defensive or apologetic about scope or timeline.
  • Loud. We earn attention through the work, not the volume.

7. Don’ts

What we won’t do. Ever.

  • Use emojis in body copy. (Permissible in inline navigation chrome only.)
  • Use stock photography of laptops, handshakes, or diverse-team-pointing-at-screen tropes.
  • Use the accent rust color (#8B3A1F) on more than one element per fold.
  • Use the wordmark smaller than 16px tall, or the lockup smaller than 32px tall.
  • Place the mark on backgrounds outside the brand palette without an explicit color override.
  • Italicize anything other than the editorial serif italic for emphasis.
