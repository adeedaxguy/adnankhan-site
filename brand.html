A personal identity for connected web craft

The Adnan Khan portfolio uses a compact mark, high-contrast editorial typography, warm neutral surfaces, violet structure, and aqua signals to connect strategy, design, and engineering.

Mark

Three stacked elements suggest structure, interface, and movement without borrowing a client logo or an agency monogram.

Typography

Large direct headlines establish the decision; restrained body typography slows the page down where explanation and proof matter.

Colour

Black and warm paper create the working surface. Violet marks structure and action, while aqua is used sparingly for confirmation and focus.

Interaction

Motion supports orientation and feedback. It should not delay reading, hide content, or ignore reduced-motion preferences.
