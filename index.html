Questions worth answering before the work begins.

Why work with Adnan instead of a larger agency?

Senior ownership stays close to the work. You deal directly with the person planning, designing, building, QAing, and improving the website—without account layers or junior handoffs.

How is the timeline decided?

The timeline follows the smallest complete release that can meet the business goal and pass QA. Anything outside that path becomes a documented next phase.

How does project scope work?

You get a written scope, launch path, milestones, responsibilities, and next-phase recommendations before work begins.

What happens after launch?

Every project includes thirty days of post-launch support for bug fixes, real-traffic tuning, and performance maintenance.
