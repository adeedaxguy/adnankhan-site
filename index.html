Questions every founder asks first

The honest answers, in order of how often I hear them.

Why work with Lofts Studio instead of a larger agency? +
Because senior ownership stays close to the work. You deal with the people planning, designing, building, QAing, and improving the website — without account layers or junior handoffs. The trade-off is focus: we take fewer active builds and keep each one documented.
Can you really launch in two weeks? +
For a build with a clear scope — a store, a marketing site, or a custom app — yes, when the first release is deliberately scoped. The trick isn’t speed. It’s a battle-tested process and the discipline to say no to things that belong in v2. We’ll define exactly what ships first, and everything else moves to a clear roadmap. No scope creep is the actual product.
How does project scope work before we start? +
Every project starts with a private URL or brief review. You get a written scope, launch path, milestones, owners, and next-phase recommendations before work begins, so both sides know exactly what is being built first.
How do you handle capacity with a two-founder studio? +
We limit active builds, assign a senior owner to each workstream, document decisions, and keep daily written or Loom updates. If a project expands, the extra work becomes a clear next phase rather than hidden drift inside the current build.
Can I see relevant work before contacting? +
Yes. The portfolio is organized by platform and category, with public launched work across Shopify, WooCommerce, WordPress, Webflow, SaaS websites, marketplaces, finance, travel, food, healthcare, real estate, and custom web applications.
How do remote agreements, IP, and handoff work? +
Scope, deliverables, credentials, access notes, staging links, launch checks, and handoff notes stay documented in the project workspace. For clients who need formal paperwork, we work through their standard agreement or a verified project workspace where appropriate.
Do you work with US and UK clients remotely? +
Yes. Most of the work is with US and UK founders. Async-first means written updates, Loom walkthroughs, documented milestones, and project workspaces that keep access, decisions, and delivery history clear.
What happens after the site goes live? +
Every project includes thirty days of post-launch support — bug fixes, real-traffic tuning, performance maintenance. After that most clients move to a lightweight ongoing support plan so I’m on call for new sections, A/B tests, and seasonal launches. About a third of my work in any given month is for a client I built for last year, or the year before.
Why is your site so quiet about “hire me”? +
Because I’d rather be the person you remembered when you needed work that lasts, than the person you bought from because the CTA was loud. If the writing here resonates, you already know where the form is. If it doesn’t, you saved both of us a call.
