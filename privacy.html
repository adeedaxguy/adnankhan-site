Privacy and data handling

This local portfolio does not run advertising analytics, collect contact details, or provide a public enquiry form. The two browser tools process only the public URL a visitor chooses to test.

Diagnostic tools

The website audit sends the entered public URL to the local audit endpoint and returns the report in the browser. The SEO/AEO checker uses the same public-page process.

Upwork

Links to Upwork lead to a separate service governed by its own privacy terms. Projects that begin there keep pre-contract communication and payment on-platform.

Project information

Information shared after a contract begins is used to scope, deliver, support, and document that engagement. This portfolio does not receive payment-card or bank details.

Future changes

This page must be reviewed before enabling analytics, marketing cookies, a CRM, or another data-collection feature on the future domain.
